Everything a 2D rig needs

Rigging

Bones with shear and inheritance, slots and draw order, skins with their own bones and constraints, IK, transform and path constraints, bounding boxes and clipping.

Meshes and weights

Edit meshes or trace them from an image's alpha, paint weights, link meshes and key deforms.

Animation

A dopesheet and a bezier graph editor, ease presets, retiming, motion paths, onion skinning, crossfade preview, events with audio and reference footage for rotoscoping.

Tools

Auto-rig, physics baking, mirror and pose tools, frame sequences, PSD and OpenRaster import with live resync, command search, customisable shortcuts and themes.

File formats

Opens skeletal animation JSON, binary and editor project files. Exports JSON or binary .skel with a packed .atlas.

Export

A texture packer and unpacker; GIF, APNG, WebP, MP4, AVI, MOV, PSD and image sequences; and a Unity bridge that exports straight into a spine-unity project.

An FX builder in the box

Pick one of 37 parametric effects, such as impacts, slashes, explosions, coin bursts, hearts and level-ups. Tune it with sliders and export it as a skeleton you can drop into a character or straight into your game. Register effects from your own Spine exports as new templates.
